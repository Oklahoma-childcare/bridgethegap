✳GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE
YOU GO.

Here are a few essentials. For details that may change, a call is always the most reliable next step.

01What days is the program open?

Oklahoma Human Services currently lists Bridge The Gap STEM Program as open on Saturday, 9:00 AM–2:00 PM, and closed Sunday through Friday.

02What ages are accepted?

The state listing identifies preschool (24–48 months / about 2–4 years) and school-age (5 years and older) categories. Contact the program for exact age eligibility and openings.

03How do I ask about an available place?

Call (405) 283-6650 or email bbillsbtgp@yahoo.com for availability, enrollment steps, and current expectations.

04What will my child work on?

STEM stands for science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Ask the program directly about the projects and activities planned for upcoming Saturdays.

05Is transportation or food included?

Those services are not confirmed by the public listing. Please ask the program directly before planning your visit.

06Where can I verify the child care license?

Oklahoma Human Services lists the program under childcare license K830058355. A link to that public record appears in the footer.
