A LOCAL PLACE FOR BIG QUESTIONS.

Bridge The Gap STEM Program is a licensed child care center in Hugo, Oklahoma, with Saturday operating hours. Its name points toward exploration, creative problem-solving, and the curiosity at the heart of STEM.

The Oklahoma Child Care Locator lists Evangela Bills as director and accepts preschool and school-age children. For firsthand details about the program's current activities and enrollment, contact the team.
