WHAT DOES
STEM MEAN?

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. Each invites a different kind of question—and opens up another way to explore the world.

Here are the ideas behind STEM. For the current Saturday activity plan, contact the team directly.

S
STEM / SCIENCE

What do you notice?

Looking closely, asking questions and making observations are building blocks of scientific thinking.

T
STEM / TECHNOLOGY

What could help?

Technology invites children to think about tools, creative solutions and how things work.

E
STEM / ENGINEERING

What could you make?

Design, testing and trying again are natural parts of building and problem-solving.

M
STEM / MATHEMATICS

Can you find a pattern?

Counting, shapes and patterns help young minds make sense of their world.
